Nkwapa is a contemporary EMR built for clinics managing hypertension and diabetes in field conditions. It covers structured encounters, prescriptions, role-based access, and sync that keeps working when the network does not. Built for the Akomapa Health Foundation, the architecture favors clinic-scoped records and an immutable audit trail so clinicians stay focused on outcomes, not infrastructure.
