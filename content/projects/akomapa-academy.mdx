Akomapa Academy is the learning home of GHELP, the Akomapa Global Health Education & Leadership Program. Students practice community-rooted care under expert mentors across a ten-course arc, from orientation and ethics through student-led clinics and reflection. The platform measures growth with pre- and post-tests, hosts course forums, and issues certificates with verifiable IDs. Built for Akomapa Health Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit serving student leaders across four countries.
